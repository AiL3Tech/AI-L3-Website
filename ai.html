Example build

How we’d set this up for a company like yours

Intake that doesn’t wait for Monday

A small litigation practice where web and phone inquiries sit in a shared inbox until someone has time. Conflict checks are manual, and promising leads go cold.

Trigger
A new inquiry arrives through the web form, email, or a call transcript.
Source
The inquiry, the practice’s matter list, and its intake criteria.
Action
Claude pulls out the parties, matter type, and key dates, flags possible conflicts against existing matters, and drafts a first response in the practice’s voice.
Output
An intake summary with a conflict flag, plus a draft reply ready for attorney review.
Who reads it
The intake attorney. Nothing goes out without them.

Designed toget same-day responses to every inquiry and give the practice a consistent conflict screen before anyone spends time on a call.

Example build

How we’d set this up for a company like yours

The document chase, automated

A tax and bookkeeping practice spends busy season emailing clients for missing statements and tracking who sent what in a spreadsheet.

Trigger
Weekly during busy season, and whenever a client uploads documents.
Source
The engagement checklist per client and the documents actually received.
Action
Claude compares what’s in against what’s required, identifies gaps, and drafts a personalized follow-up for each client.
Output
A status board showing who’s complete and who’s missing what, plus queued reminder emails.
Who reads it
The staff accountant, who approves the reminders in one pass.

Designed totake the document chase off senior staff and surface stalled engagements before the deadline, not after.

Example build

How we’d set this up for a company like yours

Seller updates that write themselves

An agent with a dozen active listings rebuilds the same weekly seller update by hand from showing feedback, portal views, and market activity.

Trigger
Every Friday for each active listing.
Source
Showing feedback, listing activity, and new comparable sales.
Action
Claude summarizes the week, highlights patterns in buyer feedback, and drafts talking points on pricing or presentation.
Output
A seller update in the agent’s voice, plus a short internal note flagging listings that may need a price conversation.
Who reads it
The agent, who reviews and sends.

Designed tokeep weekly updates consistent across every listing and hand the agent better material for the hard conversations.

Example build

How we’d set this up for a company like yours

Renewals that don’t slip

A landscaping company with seasonal maintenance contracts renews them by memory each winter, and some customers drift to competitors before anyone calls.

Trigger
60 days before each contract ends.
Source
The customer list and last season’s services.
Action
Claude drafts a personalized renewal with any suggested upgrades.
Output
Renewal emails queued, plus a list of customers who haven’t responded.
Who reads it
The office manager, who approves and sends.

Designed tolock in next season’s revenue before competitors start calling.
