05 / Straight answers

What owners
ask first.

The questions that come up on every assessment call, answered the way we’d answer them on the call.

How can a small business start using AI?

Start with the work your team repeats every week, not with a tool. An assessment identifies the tasks where AI saves the most time, then those tasks get built into the systems you already use, with your team, so they actually get used.

What is an AI assessment?

An AI assessment is a working review of how your business runs. We map your recurring tasks, find where AI saves the most time, and deliver a scoped plan that defines each workflow’s trigger, source, action, output, and who reviews it.

Do we need technical staff to use this?

No. We build it around how your team already works and hand it over with documentation and training. Your people use it in plain language.

What happens after the build?

We stay with you. As your business changes, we tune what’s running, add new workflows, and fix what breaks.
