You’re unsubscribed.

Your email address has been removed from the marketing email list for AI L3 Tech Corporation. You opted out of marketing communications, and we have recorded that choice.

When it takes effect

Immediately, in almost every case. Federal law under the CAN-SPAM Act gives a sender up to ten business days to honor an opt-out, so a message already queued before you clicked could still reach you inside that window. After that, no further marketing email will be sent to your address.

What you will still receive

This opt-out covers marketing email. It does not stop messages we send to conduct business you have asked us for: replies to your own inquiries, confirmations for calls you book, and documents such as contracts, statements of work and invoices. Those are transactional, not promotional, and an opt-out does not apply to them.

If you would also like to stop text messages or calls, email us and say so, and we will add that to your record.

If this was a mistake, or was not you

Email info@ail3tech.com and we will put it right. If someone else entered your address, tell us and we will remove it rather than ask you to confirm anything.

Your other privacy rights

Unsubscribing stops marketing email. It is separate from your rights to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold, and from any request not to have it sold or shared, which we do not do. To exercise any of those, email info@ail3tech.com with the subject line “Privacy Request”. Our Privacy Policy sets out what we collect, how long we keep it, and how to ask.
