02 / The tiers, plainly

What is tier 3
IT support?

Tier 3 IT support (also called level 3 or L3) is the highest escalation tier in a support operation: the complex, senior-engineer work that frontline technicians can’t resolve. Think broken hybrid Exchange, Entra ID and Conditional Access conflicts, Azure tenant misconfigurations, and network failures that “should work.”

In most 5–15 person MSPs there is no tier 3 team — there’s the owner. MSP escalation support means renting that tier instead: an outside senior engineering bench that takes the escalations your L1/L2 can’t close.

Support tiers, who handles them, and typical work
Tier Who handles it Typical work Example
L1 Helpdesk techs Password resets, known fixes, triage “Outlook won’t open”
L2 Experienced techs Deeper troubleshooting, escalation prep “Outlook won’t open — for the whole office”
L3 Senior engineers Root cause in complex systems, architecture, migrations “Hybrid Exchange free/busy is broken after a botched cutover”

07 / Straight answers

What MSP owners
ask first.

Six questions we get on every first call, answered the way we’d answer them on the call.

What counts as an L3 escalation?

Anything your L1/L2 can’t close: Microsoft 365 and Exchange breakage, Entra ID and Conditional Access conflicts, Azure tenant issues, Intune failures, and complex networking. If you’d normally handle it yourself, escalate it. We confirm whether it’s ours within one business hour.

How fast do you respond?

We confirm ownership of an escalation within one business hour, then work it to resolution. We operate business hours and say so honestly — no fake 24/7. If we miss the SLA window, we credit your next month.

Do you work under our brand?

Your clients stay yours. Escalations run behind your helpdesk, invisible to end clients; client-facing project work is delivered under your brand with white-label project management. We never market to your clients.

Who actually does the work?

A dedicated pod of qualified engineers, led and quality-controlled by a senior L3 supervisor. We staff each pod with the right people for the work — the difference is seniority, supervision, and documentation discipline, not geography.

What does it cost compared to hiring?

Plans start at $2,500/month for 10 included senior-engineer hours; a full-time senior engineer runs about $150K a year all-in. Most 5–15 person MSPs can’t pin that salary to any one client’s MRR — a monthly bench they can size up or down pins to all of them.

Does this replace our helpdesk or PSA?

No. Your team keeps working your PSA exactly as they do now, and decides what to escalate. We add a Slack channel and an email address on top — that’s the whole integration.
