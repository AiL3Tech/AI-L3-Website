08 / Straight answers

What MSP owners
ask first.

The questions that come up on every first partner call, answered the way we’d answer them on the call.

What is white-label L3 support?

White-label L3 support means a senior engineering team works on your MSP’s escalations and projects under your brand. Your clients deal with your company; AI L3 Tech engineers do the advanced work behind the scenes and document it for your team.

Will our clients know AI L3 Tech is involved?

No, unless you choose to tell them. We work as an extension of your team, under your name and your process.

What kinds of issues do you take on?

The L3 work your L1 and L2 technicians escalate: Intune and Conditional Access, Exchange and hybrid mail, Azure, tenant issues, mailbox migrations, and Server-to-Cloud projects. AI projects for your clients run through the Claude Launch Program.

How fast do you respond to escalations?

We confirm an escalation is ours within one business hour. Committed response times are 4 hours on Essential, 2 hours on Standard, and 1 hour on Scale.

Do you offer 24/7 coverage?

No. We cover business hours and say so plainly. If we miss the response window in your plan, we credit your next month.

How do we escalate a ticket?

Your team posts it in a dedicated Slack channel, with email as backup. Every hour we spend is visible in a live dashboard, with a monthly report on the first business day.
